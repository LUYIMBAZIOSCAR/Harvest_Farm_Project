Our Agricultural Activities

Poultry Farming

Responsible care, disease prevention, efficient housing, and productivity-focused flock management.

Goat Farming

Healthy goat rearing methods that support feeding, breeding, health, and business viability.

Dairy Farming

Best practices in herd management, milk hygiene, nutrition, and sustainable dairy production.

Banana Production

Sustainable plantation management, crop health, and long-term productivity for banana farming.
